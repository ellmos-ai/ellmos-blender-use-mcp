## Level 1 SBOM Invariant Verification (Stand: 2026-10-02)

All 10 architectural and runtime governance invariants are confirmed:
- INV-LOCAL-01: 100% Local-First & Zero-Egress (no outbound connections, telemetry, or cloud dependencies)
- INV-HEADLESS-02: Stateless & Add-on-Free (ephemeral blender --background subprocesses, no persistent daemons)
- INV-SEC-03: Non-Elevation & RunAsInvoker Principle (unprivileged user-mode execution, zero admin rights required)
- INV-BOUND-04: Timeout & Tail Buffer Bounds (bounded process runtime, 8 KB tail buffers preventing memory spikes)
- INV-INTEG-05: Deterministic JSON QA (reproducible structured reports for mesh, materials, and hierarchy)
- INV-VISUAL-06: 4-View Geometry Verification (four-angle rendering catching depth, rotation, and pivot flaws)
- INV-CLEAN-07: Fail-Closed Cleanup (guaranteed purge of ephemeral scripts and temporary render output)
- INV-CROSS-08: Cross-Platform OS Parity (verified on Windows, Linux, and macOS across Node.js 18-24)
- INV-SYNC-09: Cloud-Sync & Lock Discipline (multi-host conflict copy & canonical lock prevention in .gitignore)
- INV-SLA-10: 48h Response, 5-Day Triage & 30-Day Remediation SLA (binding security commitments in SECURITY.md)
